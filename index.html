The Project

About Odditi

Odditi is an audio-visual exploration navigating the surreal intersections of Psy-Bass, Halftime, and intricate Glitch sound design.

Blending heavy, hypnotic sub-frequencies with playful, off-kilter rhythms and organic field recordings, the project crafts sonic environments that feel both alien and strangely familiar. Every track is built as a story—shifting between deep meditative grooves and high-energy dancefloor mutations.
